# Partner research: who already owns your customer?

For B2B2C and B2C startups, the fastest channel is often an organization your buyers already trust: publishers, retailers, associations, care providers, communities, agencies, platforms.

## Steps

1. **Describe the end customer** in one sentence (from `output/icp.md`).
2. **List where they already are:** organizations they pay, trust, read, or belong to. Aim for 15 to 30 candidates, with websites. Use web search.
3. **Score each partner (1 to 5)** on:
   - reach to our exact end customer
   - why they would care (revenue share, value for their members, brand fit, filling a gap)
   - ease: do we have a warm path, are they small enough to say yes fast?
4. **Pick the top 5** and find the right person (partnerships, business development, program or product lead).
5. **Write the partner pitch:** their situation, what their members/customers get, what they get (revenue, retention, content), and a small pilot ask.

## Output: `output/partners.md`
- Table: partner, website, reach, why they care, ease, score, contact
- Top 5 with the person to approach and the warm path
- Partner pitch (50 to 70 words, scenario first, one small pilot ask)
