# Market map: count before you choose

Goal: know how many organizations can actually buy, and list them by name when the market is small. The number decides the motion.

## Steps

1. **Define the buying unit.** Who signs? A company, a department, a household, a public institution? Count that unit, not people.
2. **Estimate the count** for DACH (or your launch market). Use public lists: industry associations, registers, directories, member lists, rankings, broadcaster supplier lists, trade fair exhibitor lists. Show your sources and your math.
3. **Pick the motion from the number:**

   | Accounts | Motion |
   |---|---|
   | under ~300 | Account-based: list every account by name, research each, map buyer and user, warm intros + events + personal outreach |
   | ~300 to 5,000 | Segments: split by size, region or trigger, test 25 to 50 accounts per segment |
   | more than 5,000 | Volume: signal-based outbound plus scalable channels |
   | B2C | Count the reachable places instead: communities, partners, publications, associations where buyers already gather |

4. **For small markets, list the accounts.** Write `output/accounts.csv` with: `company, website, segment, city, why_fit, known_contact, warm_path` (who in our network can intro). Use web search; be honest about gaps.
5. **Tier them.** Tier 1 (best fit + warm path), Tier 2 (best fit, cold), Tier 3 (maybe later).

## Output: `output/market-map.md`

- Buying unit and count, with sources
- The motion that fits, in two sentences
- Top 10 Tier 1 accounts (or communities/partners for B2C)
- What we still don't know
