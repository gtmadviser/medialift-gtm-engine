# Live walkthrough (workshop Block 4, ~25 min)

A talk track for showing, live, how an agent supports a founder from strategy to a first campaign, on one team's real case. Pick a volunteer team before the block starts.

**Honest framing:** the agent does research, lists, drafts and test setup. The founder keeps the judgment: who to talk to, what to offer, what to say. Show what is real and say what still needs a human.

## Flow

1. **Context (2 min).** Paste the team's product, buyer and stall into the agent: `read CLAUDE.md. Our product is ___ ...`
2. **Market map (5 min).** `prompts/market-map.md`: count the buying unit, pick the motion from the number, list the first Tier 1 accounts. Point out how a small market changes everything downstream.
3. **One account, deep (5 min).** `prompts/account-research.md` on one Tier 1 account: trigger, buyer vs user, fit score.
4. **Positioning + first yes (5 min).** `prompts/positioning.md` and `prompts/offer-pricing.md`: one sentence, one small paid next step.
5. **Message pair (5 min).** `prompts/messaging-ab.md`: variant A and B differ in one thing. Check the quality gates out loud: scenario first, no fake stats, one soft ask, a first line that could only go to this person.
6. **Optional: stage it (3 min).** With keys: source 20 contacts and push to a *paused* lemlist campaign. Emphasize paused.

## Then: hands-on (20 min)
Every team runs one skill on their own case, Claude Code / Codex or `chatgpt/prompts.md`. Goal: one real artifact per team.

Slides and agenda: `media-lift.gtmadviser.com` and `/slides`.
