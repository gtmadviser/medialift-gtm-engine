# Example output: an account briefing

A fictional briefing to show the **shape**, not a script. Here the operator sells
**CarbonLedger**, a carbon-accounting + CSRD-reporting tool for mid-size
manufacturers, and asked the agent to research a target account. Names are
invented.

---

## output/account-nordwind-werke.md

```markdown
# Nordwind-Werke GmbH: account briefing
Fit score: 4/5 · Priority: high · Researched: 2026-06-29

## What they do
Mid-size manufacturer of industrial pumps and drive systems, ~320 staff, HQ
Bielefeld (DE), sells to machinery and water-utility OEMs across the EU.
Family-owned, no PE. Exports >50% of revenue.

## Why now (signals)
- Posted two roles in May: "Nachhaltigkeitsmanager (m/w/d)" and "Controller ESG"
 : careers page, dated 2026-05.
- LinkedIn post from the CFO about "preparing for our first CSRD report cycle"
 : 2026-06-04.
- Just above the CSRD employee/turnover threshold for FY2025 reporting: public
  Handelsregister figures.

## Where we fit
Their first CSRD cycle is the trigger. The pain is Scope 3 across an export-heavy
supply chain, today almost certainly tracked in spreadsheets. CarbonLedger's
supplier-data collection + audit-ready CSRD export is the one use case that matters
to this account right now.

## Who to reach
- A. Brand, CFO: linkedin.com/in/…: open here: she owns the CSRD mandate and
  posted about it publicly, warmest entry point.
- New Nachhaltigkeitsmanager (once hired): will be the day-to-day champion.
- T. Reuter, Head of Procurement: relevant for the Scope 3 / supplier-data angle.

## Fit + disqualifiers
Strong: right size, clear regulatory trigger, an exec already talking about it.
Watch: if they buy a big-4 advisory package they may not want software, qualify
for build-vs-buy on the first call.

## Next action + opener
Connect with the CFO on LinkedIn, then a short DM. Soft ask: a 15-min look at how
peers run their first CSRD cycle.

> Saw your note about Nordwind's first CSRD cycle. The part that usually eats the
> timeline is Scope 3 across export suppliers, most teams start in spreadsheets and
> redo it once. Happy to share how a few manufacturers your size set it up so it's
> audit-ready the first time, worth a quick look?
```

Note the rules in action: a real trigger with sources, an honest fit score with a
disqualifier, named people with the reason to open there, and an opener that is
scenario-first, under 70 words, one soft CTA, no em-dashes, no invented stats.
